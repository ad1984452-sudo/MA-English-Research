# Jacques Derrida — Deconstruction

## 1. Basic Information
- **Thinker:** Jacques Derrida
- **Period:** 20th-century French philosophy
- **Major work:** *Of Grammatology* (1967)
- **Central concern:** Questioning fixed meanings and stable interpretations in language and texts.

## 2. Key Concepts

### Binary Oppositions
Pairs such as speech/writing, presence/absence, nature/culture and reason/emotion. Traditionally, one term is often privileged over the other.

### Logocentrism
The tendency to privilege a supposedly stable source of meaning, such as speech, reason or authorial intention.

### Différance
Meaning is produced through differences between signs and is continually deferred rather than finally fixed.

### Instability of Meaning
A text may contain tensions, contradictions or assumptions that complicate its apparent central meaning.

## 3. How to Apply Deconstruction
1. Identify the text's central argument or apparent meaning.
2. Locate its binary oppositions.
3. Examine which side is privileged.
4. Look for contradictions or moments that undermine this hierarchy.
5. Develop an alternative interpretation that reveals the instability of the text's meaning.

## 4. Questions for Further Research
- Does a text have one final meaning?
- Can the marginalised term challenge the privileged term?
- How does language complicate the author's apparent intention?
